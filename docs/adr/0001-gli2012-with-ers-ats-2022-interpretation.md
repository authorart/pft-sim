# GLI-2012 reference equations with ERS/ATS 2022 interpretation rules

We use GLI-2012 (ethnic-specific, South-East Asian default for the Thai audience) as the default reference equations even though ERS/ATS 2022 recommends race-neutral GLI Global (2023); GLI Global is offered as a teaching toggle. Interpretation follows ERS/ATS 2022 (z-score severity, BDR > 10 % of predicted) by default, with a Legacy (ATS/ERS 2005: %-predicted severity, ≥ 12 % and ≥ 200 mL BDR) toggle so learners can compare the rules they will still meet in practice.
